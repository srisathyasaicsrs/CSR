Privacy Policy — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

This portal processes digital personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023), published by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). The District CSR Facilitation Cell, District Collectorate, Sri Sathya Sai District, Government of Andhra Pradesh, acts as the Data Fiduciary (Section 2(i)).

Lawful basis (Sections 4 & 6): Personal data submitted through Expression of Interest / proposal forms is processed only after free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous consent given by clear affirmative action, limited to data necessary for CSR facilitation. Processing may also occur for certain legitimate uses of the State under Section 7 (for example, performance of functions under law or provision of prescribed benefits or services by State instrumentalities).

Notice (Section 5): Before or at the time of seeking consent, you are informed of (i) the personal data and purpose of processing; (ii) how to exercise rights under Sections 6(4) and 13; and (iii) how to complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Notice content is also available in English on this portal; Telugu interface support is provided where available.

Data collected for CSR proposals: contact person name, official email, phone, company/foundation name, sector preference, proposed outlay, and optional project location details. Technical logs (IP address, browser type, date/time, pages visited) may be retained for security and audit; we do not link these to individuals unless misuse or damage to the site is detected.

Fiduciary obligations (Section 8): We implement reasonable security safeguards, process data for the specified purpose only, erase or stop processing when consent is withdrawn (unless retention is required by law), publish grievance contact details, and maintain an effective grievance redressal mechanism. Personal data is not sold or shared with unauthorised third parties for advertising.

Rights of Data Principals (Sections 11–14): right to access a summary of personal data and processing; right to correction, completion, updating and erasure; right to withdraw consent with comparable ease; right to grievance redressal; and right to nominate another individual in the event of death or incapacity.

Duties of Data Principals (Section 15): do not impersonate another person; do not suppress material information; do not register false or frivolous grievances; and furnish only verifiably authentic information when seeking correction or erasure.

Full statutory notice, DPO / grievance contacts, and withdrawal process: Privacy Policy & DPDP Notice. Official Act text (MeitY PDF): DPDP Act, 2023.

Archival Policy

District specific content published in the District Website are generic in nature, having no specific life (Time To Leave). Hence are always live and can be accessible through the website. However, content published under the sections like Events, Tenders, Recruitment and Announcements have a life span and will automatically be moved to the online archival section after the assigned end date (displayed along with each content item). Personal data retained for CSR proposals follows purpose limitation and erasure rules under Section 8 of the DPDP Act, 2023.
