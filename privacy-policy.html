Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023)

Data Privacy & Protection Policy

Official statement on digital personal data processing, affirmative consent requirements, and Data Principal statutory rights under the Government of India DPDP Act 2023.

1. Data Fiduciary Identity & Scope

This portal is operated by the District CSR Facilitation Cell, District Collectorate, Sri Sathya Sai District, Government of Andhra Pradesh ("District Administration"), acting as the Data Fiduciary under Section 2(i) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023), as published by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

This policy governs all digital personal data collected, stored, or processed through this web portal for corporate social responsibility (CSR) project proposals, single-window clearances, and partner registration, consistent with Sections 3 and 4 of the Act.

Official statute: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (PDF) · Also see Website Policies — Privacy.

2. Personal Data Collected & Lawful Purpose

In accordance to the DPDP Act 2023, notice is hereby provided that we collect the following personal data elements when an Expression of Interest (EoI) or proposal is submitted:

  • Personal Identifiers: Contact Person Full Name, Official Email Address, Contact Phone Number.
  • Corporate Identifiers: Company / Foundation Name, Sector Preference, Financial Outlay Estimates, and Project Location Preferences.
  • Technical Audit Data: Consent Timestamp and Submission Reference Identifiers.

Specified Purpose: Personal data is processed exclusively for verifying corporate credentials, facilitating single-window government clearances, field inspection scheduling, MoU execution, and communicating official district CSR project updates.

3. Consent & Free Affirmative Action (Section 6)

Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, signified by a clear affirmative action (checking the statutory consent box before form submission), and limited to personal data necessary for the specified CSR facilitation purpose (Section 6(1)).

You may withdraw consent at any time with ease comparable to giving it (Section 6(4)). Withdrawal does not affect the legality of processing already completed before withdrawal. After withdrawal, the Data Fiduciary shall cease processing within a reasonable time unless another lawful basis under the Act applies (Section 6(6)).

Personal data will never be sold, rented, monetized, or shared with third-party advertisers or unauthorised private entities.

3A. Notice Accompanying Consent (Section 5)

Every request for consent is accompanied or preceded by notice informing you of:

  • the personal data and the purpose for which it is proposed to be processed;
  • the manner of exercising rights under Section 6(4) (withdrawal) and Section 13 (grievance); and
  • the manner of making a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

Notice is available in English on this portal. Where the portal provides a Telugu interface, key consent and notice content may also be accessed in Telugu (Section 5(3) / Eighth Schedule languages).

4. Statutory Rights of Data Principals

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023, you enjoy the following enforceable rights:

Right to Access
Obtain a summary of your personal data processed by the Collectorate and information regarding processing activities.
Right to Correction & Updating
Request correction of inaccurate personal data, completion of incomplete fields, or updating of details.
Right to Erasure & Consent Withdrawal
Withdraw consent at any time and request permanent erasure of your personal data from the database.
Right to Nominate
Nominate an authorized individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

5. How to Withdraw Consent or Request Data Erasure

To exercise your Right to Erasure or Withdraw Consent under the DPDP Act 2023, you may submit a request through any of the following channels:

  1. Written Email Request: Email our designated Data Protection Officer at nodal-csr@ap.gov.in or collector-sssai@ap.gov.in specifying your proposal reference ID.

Upon receipt of a valid request, the District Administration shall within a reasonable time permanently erase your personal data from active databases and instruct Data Processors accordingly.

6. Security Safeguards (Section 8)

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, or breach (Sections 8(4)–8(5)). User login credentials are protected using salted password hashing (PBKDF2), session cookies are HttpOnly/Secure, and data transmissions use TLS.

In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Fiduciary shall intimate the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals in the form and manner prescribed (Section 8(6)).

6A. Duties of Data Principals (Section 15)

  • Comply with applicable laws while exercising rights under the Act;
  • Do not impersonate another person when providing personal data;
  • Do not suppress material information for identity or address proofs issued by the State;
  • Do not register false or frivolous grievances or complaints; and
  • Furnish only verifiably authentic information when seeking correction or erasure.
